# Research intake — WIN/WDO cross-asset regime relationship

Investigate as a new independent family, without modifying H1/H31 and without retroactive prospective credit, the WIN/WDO (Ibovespa/BRL) relationship. Re-evaluate the recently observed inverse relationship over multiple historical horizons, explicitly testing regime breaks. Test rolling correlation, lead/lag, conditional dependence and out-of-sample predictive capacity; separate discovery, validation and prospective phases. Seek ex-ante observable rules identifying regimes in which WIN up / WDO down or WIN down / WDO up has subsequent economic expectation including costs. Compare a static inverse-correlation rule against a regime-conditioned model. Do not promote contemporaneous correlation without predictive power. Reject if the family does not survive robustly across long horizons and distinct regimes.
