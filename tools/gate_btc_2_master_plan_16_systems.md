## Factory placement — official reconciliation

### Strategy Factory — discovery owner

The Strategy Factory is a cross-cutting research layer, principally spanning systems 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15 and later 16. Its mission is discovery, not self-approval:

`PREREG → SOURCE-QA → VALIDATION → ECONOMICS → INDEPENDENT REPLICATION → SURVIVAL DECISION → PROSPECTIVE SHADOW`

A survivor is frozen before scientific evidence adjudication. Negative evidence is preserved; the Factory must not retune a failed frozen hypothesis into survival.

### Evidence Factory — evidence adjudication owner

Evidence Factory receives only already-frozen candidates and applies the evidence sequence:

`PIT → SURVIVORSHIP → LEAKAGE_CAUSALITY → SOURCE_ADMISSION → DATASET_SEAL → ABLATION → SENSITIVITY_STRESS → INDEPENDENT_REPLICATION → PROSPECTIVE → ECONOMICS_READINESS → RISK_READINESS`

Allowed outcomes are `PASS`, `FAIL` or `COLLECT_MORE`. A full research pass ends at `HUMAN_PROMOTION_REVIEW`; it never activates capital or orders. EF-A0 through EF-A5 are implemented. EF-A6 autonomous orchestration remains disabled until separately proven and authorized.

### Collector Supervisor — shared operational owner

Collector Supervisor owns operational health/freshness/expected-run/append/blocker monitoring and bounded mechanical repair. It does not own scientific methodology, economics, survivor promotion or scientific clocks. Strategy Factory and Evidence Factory consume its health/counter state without taking collector ownership.
