## Frozen signal and clock

For each closed hourly candle t:

1. compute `abs(log(close_t / close_{t-1})) * 100`;
2. pass that observation to BOCPD using `StudentTModel()`;
3. use `ConstantHazard(rate=1/720)` and `max_run_length=2000`;
4. declare a change point only when the library-recommended run-length reset rule is met:
   `prev_most_likely_run_length - current_most_likely_run_length > 20`;
5. a detected change point at candle t may only affect exposure from t+1 onward.

No future candle may enter detection at t.

## Frozen economic mapping

Baseline: continuously long one unit of BTC over the evaluation window.

BOCPD overlay: same long exposure except a detected change point starts a 24-hour flat cooldown for subsequent returns. Repeated detections restart the 24-hour cooldown.

Transaction cost: 10 bps per position change (20 bps round trip). The same cost convention is applied consistently.

No leverage, no shorting, no sizing optimization.

## Frozen capability sanity check

A deterministic synthetic stream with two variance-regime breaks is generated with seed 20260921. BOCPD must produce at least one detected change point within +/-100 observations of at least one planted break. This is only a detector sanity check and carries no market-economic credit.

## Frozen real-market pass rule

The formulation passes only if all are true:

1. at least 3 and at most 100 real-market change points are detected;
2. mean absolute return over the 24 hours after detected events is at least 1.25x the unconditional mean absolute 24-hour return proxy;
3. BOCPD-overlay maximum drawdown improves by at least 10% relative to baseline drawdown magnitude;
4. BOCPD-overlay final equity after costs is at least 98% of baseline final equity after costs.

Primary status is `PASS` only if synthetic sanity and all four real-market gates pass.
